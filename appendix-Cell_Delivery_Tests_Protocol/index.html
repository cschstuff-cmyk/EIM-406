Protocol for Medium Acquisition Test

  1. Load the specified volume of distilled water into the sample reservoir.
  2. Execute the automated acquisition script.
  3. Collect the re-acquired liquid from the system output.
  4. Measure the recovered volume using calibrated instrumentation.
  5. Compute the acquisition efficiency according to Eq. (1).

\[ \text{Acquisition Rate} = \frac{\text{Recovered Volume}}{\text{Input Volume}} \tag{1} \]

The input volume assigned to each experimental group is specified in Fig. 5c.

Protocol for Cell Recovery Experiments

  1. Harvest the required number of cells from culture under standard conditions.
  2. Resuspend cells to the target concentration, ensuring each sample has a final volume of 4.2 mL.
  3. Load the sample into the system and initiate the automated cell recovery script.
  4. Collect the processed cell suspension.
  5. Quantify total cell number and assess viability.
  6. Compute the recovery efficiency according to Eq. (2).

\[ \text{Recovery Rate} = \frac{\text{Output Cell Number}}{\text{Input Cell Number}} \tag{2} \]

Special Notes and Standardization

  • For all experiments within the cell delivery system, only two rows (16 wells in total) are utilized to ensure consistent loading conditions.
  • Cell quantification follows standardized counting procedures:
    • Samples are centrifuged at 300g for 5 minutes to pellet cells.
    • The supernatant is carefully removed to minimize cell loss.
    • 10 μL of cell suspension is mixed with 10 μL of trypan blue for viability assessment.
    • Cell counting is performed using a microfluidic cell counting chip.
    • Total cell number and viability are recorded for downstream analysis.
