Seepage test

Objective

Verify seepage does not occur within cargo channel set up.

Methodology

Cargo channel part was assembled with ITO plate and filled with water at alternate channels, such that liquid formed stable menisci that extended above the channel openings due to capillary effects.

Cargo channel was left on bench, with observations conducted at 30 minutes intervals (over a total duration of 1.5 hours) to identify presence of seepage.

Observations obtained were:

  1. Height of meniscus for each channel relative to surface of cargo channel walls.
  2. Taking advantage of the clarity of the clear acrylic, observed whether there were any visible changes in the cargo channel that could suggest water seepage through possible cracks. Took photos using flash at different angles to check for any abnormal reflections of light (Fig.1).
