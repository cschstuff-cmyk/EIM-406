Objective

To verify that output of pulse generator remains as a square wave, at 40Hz and 200us, at 96 wellplate configuration with OptiMEM present.

Methodology

To run Arduino-based pulse generator through cargo channel and cargo wells that are assembled and filled with OptiMEM. Pulse generator circuit is programmed to produce square waveform with target parameters of 40 V peak voltage, 200 µs pulse width, and 40 Hz frequency for 2 minutes.

Three readings were recorded for each parameter to assess repeatability. The mean value was calculated, while accuracy was evaluated using absolute and percentage error. Precision was assessed using standard deviation and coefficient of variation.

Coefficient of Variation is calculated as such:

CV(%) Variation Level
Less than 10% Low
10 - 20% Moderate
More than 20% High

Likewise, Percentage Error is classified as such:

PE(%) Error Level
Less than 10% Low
10 - 20% Moderate
More than 20% High

This methodology enables evaluation of the system's ability to maintain consistent output performance in 96-hole well plate configuration with OptiMEM present.

Observations: Peak voltage is slightly above the target of 40 V, with a low percentage error (AE = 3.33, PE = 8.33%) and low variation (CV = 0.53 %). Pulse width is slightly above 200 µs, with low variation (CV = 2.3 %). Frequency is extremely stable (40.16 Hz) with minimal deviation (CV = 0.04 %). Overall, the system maintains its square waveform and demonstrates good stability and closeness to the design targets at 40 V input.

Remarks: Peak voltage observed is lower than recorded when using resistors to simulate the load, suggesting further voltage drop possible during full transfection run.
