Simulation Protocol

We performed in silicon simulation with COMSOL multiphyics™ on electric field distribution in a single well. We assign staninless steel at the plate material, pure Titanium as the electrode material, and RPMI-1640 as the media. Setup dimension follows standard 96 well plate in in vitro experiment. We ignore the cell-induced change in media physic property. COMSOL source file can be found: here.
