Verification Test with ITO and Metal Plate

Objective

Validate that voltage drop does not occur and is delivered uniformly; Verify that set up can conduct current as expected, it can be conducted from metal plate to top surface of ITO plate, with conductive tape acting as intermediary mode of connection.

Methodology

Placed prepared ITO plate on metal plate, connected metal plate to power source as seen in Fig. 1, and obtained readings from chosen points to determine presence of voltage drop.

Each voltage reading is obtained a distance away from the centre which positive terminal is connected to, as illustrated in image X.

Recorded readings for each point, with input voltage of 5V, 10V, 20V and 40V.

Observations: Across all tested voltage inputs, the measured voltage at every point on the plate electrode was equal to the applied input voltage.

  • At 5V, the measured voltage was consistently 5V across all points.
  • At 10V, the measured voltage was consistently 10V across all points.
  • At 20V, the measured voltage was consistently 20V across all points.
  • At 40V, the measured voltage was consistently 40V across all points.

No variation was observed between different positions or repeated measurements.

No voltage drop was observed and voltage was evenly distributed, current could be conducted through set up as planned
